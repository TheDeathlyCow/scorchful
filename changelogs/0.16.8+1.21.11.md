# Scorchful 0.16.8 for Minecraft 1.21.11

* Updated embedded Immersive Storms version from `1.5.0` -> `1.6.1`

### Immersive Storms 1.6.0:

- Added a new Black Rain weather effect to Pale Gardens
    - This only affects Pale Gardens, and not other Dense Fog biomes
- Dark Forests, the Deep Dark, and other "Spooky" biomes are now affected by dense fog
- Strong wind from sandstorms and blizzards now plays slightly muffled when indoors
- Fog is now slightly less dense in most cases

### Immersive Storms 1.6.1:

* Fixed rain particles from Particle Rain not being black when in the Pale Gardens
* Fixed rain not being black in the Pale Gardens with Subtle Effects
