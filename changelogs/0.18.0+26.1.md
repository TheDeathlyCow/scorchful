# Scorchful 0.18.0 for Minecraft 26.1.x: Someone should dust in here

- Added Dust and Red Dust, replacing the default drops of Sand Piles and Red Sand Piles, respectively.
    - The Sand Pile and Red Sand Pile block items can still be obtained with a silk touch tool.
    - Dust and Rest Dust can be crafted in a 2x2 pattern to produce Sand and Red Sand, respectively.
    - Dust and Rest Dust can be smelted into Quartz
    - Husks, Parched, and Camel Husks will drop 0-2 Dust on death, increasing by up to 1 per level of looting (exactly same rate as Husks drop Rotten Flesh).
- The Fear mob effect now reduces armor by 50% per level of Fear, and recolors the armor bar to be red.
- Drowned will now randomly spawn with Copper and Turtle Armor on Normal and Hard difficulty.
    - The equipping logic is similar to how Zombies will spawn with armor.
    - Drowned are 3x more likely to spawn with Copper armor than with Turtle.
- Renamed the `doSandPileAccumulation` config option to `enableSandPileAccumulation` for consistency.
    - This change will be automatically applied to existing configs with schema version 8.
- Fixed the Red Sand Cauldron not being properly pickaxe-mineable
- Fixed full Sand Pile and Red Sand Pile blocks (8 layers) not dropping their respective full blocks.
- Fixed an incorrect title for the Scorchful Creative Mode Tab.
- Fixed Engulfed Hearts rendering incorrectly.
